CUSTOMER TRANSACTION DASHBOARD
DAX MEASURES
==============================

1. Total Customers
   = DISTINCTCOUNT(customer_data[CustomerID])


2. Total Locations
   = DISTINCTCOUNT(customer_data[Location])


3. Total Orders
   = DISTINCTCOUNT(customer_data[OrderID])


4. Total Products
   = DISTINCTCOUNT(customer_data[ProductInformation])


5. Total Transaction Amount
   = SUM(customer_data[TransactionAmount])


6. Average Order value
   = DIVIDE([Total Transaction Amount], [Total Orders])
